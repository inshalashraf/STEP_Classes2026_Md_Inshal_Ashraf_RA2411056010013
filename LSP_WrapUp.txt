FixedDepositAccount should not implement Withdrawable because it cannot honestly support early withdrawal.
Making it implement Withdrawable and throwing an exception would violate the LSP substitution rule: a subtype must be usable wherever its base type or contract is expected without breaking the caller's assumptions.
The withdrawal loop now accepts only Withdrawable accounts, so SavingsAccount and CurrentAccount can be safely substituted there.
FixedDepositAccount remains an Account, but it does not promise a withdrawal operation that it cannot fulfill.
